I.Our story

A small world, slowly made.

Terrarium Bistro is a new neighbourhood bistro in Tarporley, built around good plates, a properly poured glass and evenings that don't need rushing.

II.The name

ter·rar·i·um

/təˈreə.ri.əm/noun

A sealed glass garden. A small, self-contained world where things grow slowly and well.

That felt like the right name for a bistro. A room warm enough to forget the weather outside, food that rewards a little patience, and a table you won't want to leave.

The plate

A menu with a passport: Ottoman lamb, Spanish tapas, Italian pasta and British classics, all cooked with care.

The glass

Red, white and rosé, chosen to sit happily beside the food rather than shout over it.

The table

Warm, unhurried service that knows when to chat and when to leave your evening to you.
